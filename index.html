How RailSwap works

A transparent flow from listing to checkout.

1
Create an account

Verify your contact details and complete any required identity checks.

2
Submit ticket details

Enter permitted details through a secure form, not a public screenshot.

3
Verify eligibility

Only an authorized, reliable source can establish live booking status.

4
Secure checkout

Use an approved payment provider once the transaction is legally permitted.

🛡️

Verification comes first

RailSwap will only show “Verified” after a successful check through an authorized railway integration. This demo cannot access live railway records.

Check PNR status

Demo interaction only. No PNR is sent to a railway system.

Security principles

  • Hosted payment checkout
  • Never store raw card data
  • Signed payment webhooks
  • Duplicate submission checks
  • Restricted access to passenger data
  • Audit logs and fraud monitoring

Important notice

Indian railway tickets are subject to railway transfer, passenger-name, cancellation and resale rules. A PNR lookup alone does not make a ticket transferable. Live listing, transfer and payment features remain disabled in this prototype.
